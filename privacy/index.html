Privacy Policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

CHIANG MAI DESOC CO., LTD. operates 4seas.xyz and the 4Seas Coliving portal. This policy explains how we handle personal information on the site and in the Coliving application and member portal.

Information we collect and how we use it

When you apply to Coliving or contact us, we receive information you provide, such as your name, email address, application answers, stay requests, and messages. We use it to review applications, manage accounts and stays, and respond to your requests. Our hosting systems may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to operate and protect the service. The Coliving portal uses cookies to complete sign-in and keep you signed in.

Sign in with Google

If you choose Google sign-in, we request the OpenID Connect scopes openid, email, and profile. Google can return your account identifier, email address and verification status, display name, and profile picture URL. We use your email to match your existing Coliving application or account. When we create a member record, we store the email address, a keyed hash of the email used for matching, and your display name if provided.

The Google access token is used during sign-in to retrieve your profile. We do not retain Google tokens, your Google account identifier, or your profile picture URL in the Coliving member record. We do not request access to Gmail, Drive, Calendar, or contacts. We use Google data for authentication and account setup. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or provide it to advertisers.

Sharing and retention

Authorized 4Seas staff and service providers may process information as needed to operate, host, or support the service. We may disclose information when legally required. We retain account and application records for as long as needed to administer the service, address disputes, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Your choices and how to contact us

To request access to your information, a correction, or deletion of your account and related data, email coliving@4seas.xyz. We may need to verify your identity and retain records where required by law. You can remove 4Seas access in your Google Account connections. Removing that connection does not delete information already held by 4Seas.
